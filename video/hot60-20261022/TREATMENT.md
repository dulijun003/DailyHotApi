# TREATMENT — 热榜60秒 · 10.22 (Caption News, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 60 s)

**Brief (defaults taken, the user said "按你的思路")**: Chinese voice and captions, English second line, vertical for a feed, no storyboard stop,
material = the night's hot lists from the user's own DailyHotApi (7 lists, 378 entries) + public-domain NASA imagery + Natural Earth
physical geography. No brand footage, no news photos, no national borders drawn (a physical locator only).

## 1. Three structures

1. **Countdown of numbers** — 378 → 10 → 478万 → 2:3 → 7.0. Clear, but treats an earthquake as one more number in a row.
2. **The seven No.1s** — seven platforms, seven different firsts, a grid that lights one by one. Honest to the data, but a slide: seven equal cells, no turn.
3. **Frequency vs. weight (chosen)** — what the lists *talk about most* is a phone (4 platforms, 10 entries); two lighter stories pass;
   then a freeze: two of the lists put *the same story first*, and it is a 7.0 earthquake. The film ends by re-reading the hook's 378:
   "of 378 entries, this one weighs most — 愿平安". It has a real turn that comes out of the data itself (how often ≠ how important),
   and it lets the tone move from light to grave without a jolt.

Opening: the biggest number on frame 4 (378, rolling). Ending: the hook's number re-read, then a two-part verdict caption on the plateau after a silence.

## 2. Logline and arc

Tonight's 378 trending entries talk most about a folding phone — but the one story two lists put first is an earthquake on the Tibetan Plateau.
Setup (378 entries, the phone) → turn (freeze: "two lists, same No.1") → ending (the quake, read from the lists' own entries; 愿平安).

## 3. Benchmark

- **Vertical captions-first social news (mid-2010s explainers)** — learn: one idea per caption, a number every few seconds, source on screen, sound-off legibility, 1–3 s shots. Don't take: logos, fonts, colours, idents, any shot.
- **Election-night / disaster "locator" graphics on TV** — learn: a plain graticule with one point and its coordinates reads faster than a political map; push-in to the point, expanding ring. Don't take: any channel's map style.
