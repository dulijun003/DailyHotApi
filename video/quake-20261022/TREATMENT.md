# TREATMENT — 巴青 7.0 级地震，讲清楚 (Caption News + real material, 1080×1920, 30 fps, 65.5 s)

Brief: the approved script (one hot topic, explained), Caption News style, real licence-clean material only. No ground footage of the quake is licence-clean, so the "footage" is the day's own satellite pass, the USGS shaking estimate, NASA night and ISS imagery; every fact is re-set as type with its source on screen.

Structure (chosen of three — countdown of numbers / timeline of the night / **five questions**): where → how deep → who is near → how far → how high → which number → what followed → who is hurt → who responds → what is not known. One fact per shot; the hook is the biggest number on frame 4 (7.0), the ending returns to the night of the hook.
